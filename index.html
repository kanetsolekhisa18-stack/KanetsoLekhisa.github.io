A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity backed by quantitative thinking.

I'm driven by curiosity and a desire to use mathematics, statistics and technology to solve meaningful real-world problems.

I'm currently completing my BSc in Actuarial Science at the University of the Free State. My interests span finance, economics, insurance, statistics, business intelligence, forecasting and risk analysis.

I have programming experience in R, VBA, Python and VSS, as well as a strong working knowledge of Microsoft Excel, SQL and Microsoft Office. I enjoy exploring technology, learning new tools and building practical solutions.

Outside my professional interests, I enjoy playing the drums and piano, reading, hiking and learning about animals, plants and the natural world. I also value teaching and sharing knowledge.

I'm particularly interested in making analytical work useful to organisations: not just producing results, but explaining what those results mean.
